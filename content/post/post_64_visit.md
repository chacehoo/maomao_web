Last week, Dr. Hu visited KU Leuven, Belgium to attend the public PhD defense of Dr. Hafeez, who previously spent three months as a visiting student at the BIOR lab. During the visit, Dr. Hu also gave a seminar at the Department of Electrical Engineering titled “Decarbonization of building and urban energy systems: a multiscale data-driven approach.” The talk discussed how data-driven methods can support better design and management decisions for building and urban energy systems across multiple scales, from equipment and buildings to communities and cities.

Sincere thanks to [Prof. Geert Deconinck](https://www.esat.kuleuven.be/electa/professors/00004317) and [Prof. Hussain Kazmi](https://www.kuleuven.be/wieiswie/en/person/00107015) for their kind invitation and warm hospitality.

Huge congrats, Dr. Hafeez! We are honored to have hosted you as a visiting student at the BIOR lab and wish you all the best in your next chapter ahead.
