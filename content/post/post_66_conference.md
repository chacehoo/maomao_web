Dr. Maomao Hu participated in the [19th International Conference of the International Society of Indoor Air Quality & Climate (Indoor Air 2026)](https://www.indoorair2026.org/), held in Singapore from 14-18 June 2026.

During the conference, Dr. Hu chaired the session of Infectious Aerosols Transmission and Mitigation.

Dr. Hu also served on the Local Organising Committee, the International Scientific Committee, and the Poster Judging Panel.

It was a great opportunity to contribute to the organisation of the conference and engage with the international indoor air research community.
