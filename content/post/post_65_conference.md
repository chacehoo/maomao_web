Our BIOR Lab members had a fruitful time in Hong Kong attending the [International Conference on Building and Simulation (BAS 2026)](https://www.bas2026.org/index.html), held at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology from 11-13 June 2026.

Extremely proud of our PhD student Qinhuan Liu for presenting his work on *“Resilience-Informed Optimal PV-Battery Sizing for Grid-Responsive Building Communities Against Extreme Weather Events.”*

Dr. Hu also chaired the session on *Climate-Neutral Building Design and Assessment; Building–Grid Interaction*.

It was a wonderful opportunity to share our latest research, meet colleagues, and exchange ideas with the building simulation community.
